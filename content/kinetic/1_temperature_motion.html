Temperature IS Motion

Temperature isn't some mysterious abstract quantity. It's simply a measure of how fast the particles are moving.

T = ⅔ × (Eavg / kB)

Each particle has kinetic energy (½mv²). Temperature is the average energy of all particles, adjusted by two constants:

  • kB (Boltzmann constant) scales energy into Kelvin units.
  • ⅔ comes from the fact that particles can move in three directions (up/down, left/right, back/forward).

This is the kinetic theory of gases: temperature is motion, and motion is temperature. Watch the particles on the right; their average speed is directly proportional to the temperature of the gas.
